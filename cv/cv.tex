\aboutme{Postdoc researcher and assistant professor at the University of Córdoba, teaching \textit{\textbf{Statistics}} in the degrees of Tourism and Business Administration, \textit{\textbf{Quantitative methods in Management}} and \textit{\textbf{Microeconomics}} in the degree of business administration and \textit{\textbf{Operations Research}} in the Master of Business Administration. My dissertation was based on the development of mortality and fertility projection models and the role of longevity in financial planning for retirement. It was awarded \emph{\textbf{Cum Laude with International Mention}}.}
